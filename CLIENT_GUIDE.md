# 📘 User Guide - Francesca AI Agent V1

Welcome to the user guide for **Francesca**, your new intelligent virtual assistant on WhatsApp. This guide will help you understand how to interact with Francesca and manage her advanced features.

## ✨ What can Francesca do for you?

Francesca is not just a simple chatbot. She is designed to be an extension of your team:

1.  **Natural Conversation:** You can talk to her like a real person. She understands context and remembers your preferences.
2.  **Document Analysis:** Send her a PDF or a photo of a document. Francesca will read it, archive it, and provide a summary of the key points.
3.  **Voice Messages:** You can send her voice notes, and she will respond with her voice (Elsa). If you prefer reading, she will transcribe what you said.
4.  **Vision:** Send her a photo. She can describe it, read text within it, or analyze charts.
5.  **Memory:** She remembers past conversations. If you talked to her about a project last week, she will know what you are talking about today.

## 🚨 The Intervention System (Escalation)

Francesca is programmed to be cautious. If she receives a request she is unsure about or that requires your authorization (the "Boss"), she will send you a private message containing:
- The client's name.
- The question received.
- A proposed response.

### How to Respond to Francesca (Boss Mode)
When Francesca asks for help, you can respond simply with a number:
- **1:** To approve the request (Francesca will tell the client to proceed).
- **2:** To deny the request.
- **3:** To inform the client that you will contact them personally later.

Alternatively, you can write a custom response, and Francesca will forward it directly to the client.

## 📂 Automatic Archiving

Every document sent to Francesca is saved in a folder organized by Person and Company. Francesca automatically extracts this information from the document itself.

## 🎙️ Voice Commands

If you want Francesca to respond by voice, use phrases like:
- "Tell me out loud..."
- "Send me an audio..."
- "Let me hear..."

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

- **Francesca is not responding:** Ensure the server is running. You might need to re-scan the QR code if the session has expired.
- **"Neural Error":** This usually indicates a temporary connection issue with the AI servers. Please try again in a few moments.
